About the Conclave

Frontier science meets digital innovation

NIELIT Bio-AI Conclave 2026 (NBioAI2026) is envisaged as a high-level two-day scientific conclave bringing together academicians, researchers, government institutions, industry representatives, healthcare professionals, startups, innovators, NGOs, and students to explore the transformative potential of Artificial Intelligence, bioinformatics, computational biology, and digital research infrastructure in advancing the life sciences.

The programme will feature keynote lectures, expert panels, invited talks, industry interactions, and research poster presentations, providing a platform for scientific exchange, collaboration, and knowledge sharing. An India-centric Bio-AI roadmap will also be explored, focusing on research priorities, emerging technologies, capacity building, and translation of innovation into impactful applications.

As pre-conference activities, the event will include five workshops on emerging Bioinformatics areas, a Bio-AI Hackathon for postgraduate and doctoral students, and a Bio-AI Project Competition for Class X–XII students.

A dedicated Bio-AI Innovation Showcase will provide complimentary space to relevant industries, startups, NGOs, government organisations, and research institutions to showcase their technologies, products, research, and innovative solutions.

Scientific Tracks

Five professional Bio-AI tracks

Researchers and students are invited to contribute to the following thematic areas.

TRACK 01

Foundation Models, Generative Systems and Scientific Intelligence

Foundation models, multimodal learning, generative models, agents, scientific co-pilots, biological language models and reasoning systems.

TRACK 02

Genomics, Multi-Omics and Precision Health

Genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, spatial omics, single- cell biology, biomarkers, clinical genomics and precision medicine.

TRACK 03

Computational Biology, Bioinformatics and Research Infrastructure

HPC/GPU, cloud, scientific workflows, NGS pipelines, reproducibility, research data platforms, automation and containers.

TRACK 04

Molecular Discovery, Protein Science and Structural Biology

Protein structure/function, protein design, molecular generation, virtual screening, drug discovery, computational chemistry and drug repurposing.

TRACK 05

Digital Life Sciences, Health, Agriculture and Biodiversity

Digital health; healthcare AI; agricultural bioinformatics; AI in agriculture; environmental informatics; biodiversity applications.

Programme

Two Days of Frontier Science, Translation & Innovation

DAY 1

Frontier Science, AI & Industry

The first day will focus on the frontiers of Artificial Intelligence and Life Sciences, featuring the inaugural session, keynote address, scientific sessions on AI & Foundation Models, Genomics, Multi-Omics & Precision Medicine, research poster showcase, industry interaction, Exhibit-Bio 2026 and networking activities.

DAY 2

Translation & Innovation

The second day will focus on AI for Drug Discovery, Protein Intelligence & Structural Biology, Applied Bio-AI, Digital Health, Agriculture & Biodiversity, along with poster evaluation, young innovators, career guidance and the valedictory session.

Submission

Paper Submission

Prospective authors are invited to submit original full-length research papers and review papers presenting significant findings, methodologies, applications, systems, technologies, datasets, analytical approaches, or case studies within the designated scientific tracks of NIELIT Bio-AI Conclave 2026. All manuscripts must be original, unpublished, and not simultaneously submitted elsewhere. Submissions should be made through the designated conference submission portal and will undergo doble blind peer review based on scientific/scholarly quality, originality, relevance, methodological rigour, and clarity, as applicable to the manuscript type and scope.

Manuscripts must be submitted in English and prepared in accordance with the prescribed formatting, structure, authorship, originality, reference, declaration, and file-format requirements. Authors are requested to carefully refer to the Full-Paper Submission Guidelines for detailed instructions before submission.
