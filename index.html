About the Conclave

Frontier science meets digital innovation

NIELIT Bio-AI-2026 is proposed as a high-level two-day scientific conclave bringing together academia, research institutions, government, industry, healthcare, startups and students to examine how Artificial Intelligence, bioinformatics, computational biology and digital research infrastructure are transforming the life sciences.

The conclave will create a shared space for researchers, technology leaders, practitioners and emerging talent to exchange ideas, showcase promising work and discuss how advances in AI can be translated into meaningful solutions across genomics, drug discovery, digital health, biological data science and related domains.

The programme is deliberately structured around keynote lectures, expert panels, industry interaction, poster research, innovation and an India-focused Bio-AI roadmap rather than conventional oral-paper sessions. The emphasis is on meaningful dialogue, collaboration and practical pathways that can strengthen India’s Bio-AI research ecosystem and encourage new partnerships between science, technology and application communities.

Scientific Tracks

Five professional Bio-AI tracks

Researchers and students are invited to contribute to the following thematic areas.

TRACK 01

AI, Generative AI & Foundation Models for Life Sciences

Generative AI, LLMs, multimodal AI, foundation models, AI agents, scientific copilots and AI-assisted bioinformatics.

TRACK 02

AI-Driven Genomics, Multi-Omics & Precision Medicine

Genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, multi-omics, biomarkers, precision medicine and clinical genomics.

TRACK 03

Bioinformatics & Computational Biology Infrastructure and Applications

Cloud computing, HPC/GPU computing, research data platforms, workflow automation, NGS pipelines, Docker/Nextflow and reproducible research.

TRACK 04

AI for Drug Discovery, Protein Intelligence & Structural Biology

Protein structure/function, protein language models, molecular design, virtual screening, drug repurposing and computational chemistry.

TRACK 05

Applied Bio-AI, Digital Health, Agriculture & Biodiversity

Digital health, healthcare AI, agricultural bioinformatics, AI in agriculture, environment and biodiversity.
